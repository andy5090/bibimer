Android TTS now prepares the engine and applies device speech rate and pitch when enabled or when you return to the app. Device setting changes are detected while the app is open, without starting playback. Monitoring stops when TTS is off, the app is hidden, or an external provider is selected. Per-answer play/stop controls, full-answer and local summary modes, and default-off behavior are retained. No bridge update is needed.
